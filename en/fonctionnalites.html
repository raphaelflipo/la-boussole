Manage members, activities and rides.

La Boussole is designed to handle membership requests, invitations, administrator permissions, events, carpools and bookings. Availability depends on the version and platform.

Try it with fictional data

Before travelling

Create an activity and set a meeting point. Choose the groups involved and offer rides.

  • Departure point and destination
  • Outbound and return legs
  • Seats offered to passengers
  • People who still need a ride on either leg

Booking available seats

Enter the household’s travel needs, then choose the passengers and the leg to book. A partial cancellation frees the selected seats. If a ride is full, members can choose to join a waiting list.

  • Named bookings for multiple household passengers on each leg
  • First-come, first-served waiting list that keeps groups together
  • In-app confirmations

Managing repeat activities

Duplicate an event, plan recurring activities and archive past events.

  • Ride history
  • In-app reminders
  • Notification preferences

Features are in development and the browser demo uses fictional scenarios. Planned notifications are in-app reminders. Automatic text messages and emails have not been announced.

An interface built for phones

Version 1.0 organises the mobile app around Home, Activities, Rides, Teams and Profile. Forms open at the bottom of the screen and going back returns to the previous step. Light and dark themes and larger text are part of the iOS and Android design.

The native apps use C++ and Qt 6/QML. The web demo runs separately. See the mobile screens and test coverage.

Clear responsibilities

Owners and organisers manage membership and activities. Members see rides for their own groups.
