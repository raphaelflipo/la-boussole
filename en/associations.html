How to prepare a pilot

  1. Prepare. Appoint an adult organiser and read the pilot guide.
  2. Practise. Use fictional accounts, places and scenarios.
  3. Observe. Track which steps succeed and report blockers without sharing personal data.
  4. Decide. Address issues and verify permissions before opening the pilot.
