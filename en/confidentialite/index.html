Privacy and legal notice

This notice covers the La Boussole website, the fictional web demo and pilot enquiries sent by email.

Publisher, hosting and contact

Raphaël Flipo, Lille metropolitan area, France, publishes the site and handles enquiries. You can contact him at r.flipob@gmail.com. GitHub Pages hosts the site. GitHub’s privacy statement describes technical logs and the handling of visitor data.

Browsing and the fictional demo

The site uses no advertising cookies, trackers or audience analytics. Your browser may store your display preference. The demo uses fictional data and runs in your browser.

Enquiries about the pilot

If you email about a future pilot, I receive your address and message. I use them to respond and discuss the conditions of a possible pilot. I keep your enquiry for 12 months after the last contact. Please leave out sensitive information and information about minors.

Your rights

You can email to ask for access, correction, deletion or a restriction of processing, or to object. You may also contact the French data protection authority, the CNIL.

Native application

The connected 1.0 demo uses fictional people and locations. A dedicated privacy policy will be provided to associations before any use with real data. The original French notice describes the current tools for access, export and deletion and the work required before a pilot with real data.
