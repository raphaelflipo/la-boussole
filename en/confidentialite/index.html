Privacy and legal notice

This notice covers the La Boussole website, the fictional web demo and pilot enquiries sent by email.

Publisher, hosting and contact

Raphaël Flipo, Lille metropolitan area, France, publishes the site and handles enquiries. You can contact him at r.flipob@gmail.com. GitHub Pages hosts the site. GitHub’s privacy statement describes technical logs and the handling of visitor data.

Browsing and the fictional demo

The site uses no advertising cookies, trackers or audience analytics. Your browser may store your theme and language preferences. These preferences stay on this device. The demo uses fictional data and runs in your browser.

Enquiries about the pilot

If you email about a future pilot, I receive your address and message. I use them to respond and discuss the conditions of a possible pilot, based on the steps you request before entering into a contract and my legitimate interest in answering messages. Only I handle enquiries, using Google’s Gmail service. I keep your enquiry for 12 months after the last contact, then delete it. Please leave out sensitive information and information about minors.

Your rights

You can email to ask for access, correction, deletion or a restriction of processing, or to object. I will respond within one month. You may also contact the French data protection authority, the CNIL.

Version 1.0 and the pilot

The connected 1.0 demo uses fictional people and locations. Associations will receive a dedicated privacy policy before they begin using real data.

  • Access depends on the association, approved membership and the member’s role. An adult manages the household’s information. Drivers and organisers see the information they need for their roles.
  • A profile photo, public meeting point and directory details are optional. Members can choose and withdraw directory sharing separately for each association. The app has no continuous location tracking.
  • For associations with minors, enhanced privacy reduces which member details are visible and disables meeting points. The app does not verify parental authority or permission to transport children.
  • The connected app keeps session tokens and session data in memory. Closing the app fully requires signing in again. Exports you request are local files and contain personal data.

Access, export and deletion

You can correct profile details, request a JSON or CSV export, remove the public meeting point and request account deletion. The export covers app records. You may need a separate request for technical records such as some login metadata.

To leave an association, contact its organiser. An owner must transfer responsibility before deleting their account. Deletion does not erase copies already exported or backed up. Some records and free-text fields may need further review.

Work before a pilot with real data

Archived records are retained. There is currently no general automatic deletion schedule. Retention by data category, backup handling, controller roles and contacts, legal bases, contracts and transfer safeguards must be documented and verified before use with real data.

The connected test version uses Firebase Authentication and Firestore with an API on Render. Maps request tiles from an OpenStreetMap provider. The final app notice must identify the hosting regions and provider terms. The public website and its fictional demo are separate from the connected app.
