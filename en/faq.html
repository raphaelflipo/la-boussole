The demo

Do I need an account?

The browser demo uses three fictional associations, preset profiles and sample trips. Book or cancel a seat, offer a ride, then switch to Organiser to add a fictional activity or approve a member.

Are my changes saved or sent anywhere?

Your changes only exist in the page’s memory. The demo does not use accounts, cookies, persistent storage, trackers, network calls or third-party services. Reload the page or choose Reset to clear the scenarios.

Does this demo use the connected app?

The demo illustrates the workflow with fictional data. It runs independently of Firebase or any payment system. The connected application is tested separately.

How La Boussole works

Who is it designed for?

La Boussole is designed for associations, sports clubs, scout groups and other groups organising activities and transport. Groups still need to follow applicable transport rules and obtain insurance and permissions.

Can I join more than one association?

An account can join more than one association. Each association has its own members, events, rides and roles. Administrative access applies to the association where it was granted.

Is a subgroup a separate private space?

Subgroups organise activities within an association. Approved members share access to information in that association. Create separate associations if they need separate access boundaries.

What happens if two people book the last seat?

The intended behaviour is to prevent the seat being assigned twice. The browser demo illustrates that outcome. The connected service needs its own concurrent-booking checks.

Are reminders sent by email or text message?

The proposed Association and Organisation plans show upcoming activity reminders inside the app. Email, SMS and push reminders are outside the scope of this beta.

Plans

Can I subscribe now?

Découverte is planned as a free tier, Association at €19 a month and Organisation at €49 a month, per association. Subscription, card payment and billing are inactive. Tax information, terms and timing will be set before sales open.

Do limits reset each month?

Limits count the members, groups, events, trips and organisers currently in an association. Removing an item frees up capacity.

What are the limits?

Découverte includes 30 members, 1 group, 5 events, 20 rides and 1 organiser. Association includes 150 members, 5 groups, 100 events, 300 rides and 1 organiser. Organisation includes 500 members, 20 groups, 500 events, 1,000 rides and 5 organisers. The owner counts as an organiser.

Can I move to a smaller plan?

The local simulation blocks a downgrade if existing items or active roles exceed the lower tier’s limits. Existing data stays available. Final downgrade terms will be set before subscriptions open.

Is there a fee to export my data?

The free plan includes personal data export in JSON and CSV. It covers the personal records returned by the service. Ask separately for additional metadata or technical history. Copies already downloaded remain on your device after account deletion. Association-wide export is a separate administrative function.

Your data

Can I use real details in the demo?

Choose only the fictional options. The demo provides no free-text fields for names, addresses or messages. The app’s privacy provisions, responsibilities and retention periods need validation before a real pilot.

Can minors already have accounts?

Before onboarding minors, each association needs to determine eligibility, notice and parental permission requirements, image rights and the transport rules that apply. Preparatory tests use fictional people.

Should pickup points be home addresses?

Use public, non-sensitive places. Keep private addresses, precise locations of minors, health information and other sensitive data out of titles, rides and feedback.

Where are the privacy and legal notices?

See the legal notice, the privacy notice and the app’s draft privacy provisions. The final app policy and data processing documents need to be validated and delivered to associations before they use real data. Commercial terms will be published before subscriptions open.

Household transport in 1.0

Can passengers need different seats on the outbound and return legs?

Each passenger can have a separate need for each leg. A confirmed place, independent travel, a waiting list entry and an unstated need are separate states. Household management does not establish parental authority.

Does a waiting list add people to the total?

People on the waiting list are already included in the count of passengers without seats. For example, the dashboard can show five without seats, two of them waiting.

Can a household be split automatically?

The queue follows arrival order. If the next party needs two seats and only one opens up, they keep their place in the queue. The household can explicitly select different passengers to book.

What does pending confirmation mean?

The request was sent and the seat is still being confirmed. If the response is lost, the app keeps that request for review. Retrying reuses the same request to avoid creating a second booking.

Can I receive notices with the app closed?

Notices are available after opening and refreshing the app. Reading a notice does not accept a change. SMS, email and external alerts are not guaranteed.

Which journeys have been tested?

See the app walkthrough and original screenshots and availability by platform. The browser demo runs independently. Device checks are recorded separately.

The pilot

How will a pilot start?

After the remote environment, distribution and documents are validated, an adult organiser can prepare a first event with a small team. The aim is to try an invitation, a booking and a cancellation, then learn what caused difficulties.

Must I leave a Mac and emulators running?

The browser demo runs on its own. The remote test server is hosted separately and has been reached remotely. The free hosting may take time to wake up and continuous availability is not assured. Real-world use still needs validation.

Where can I report an issue now?

The demo’s feedback button displays a preset message locally. The pilot will provide a verified support contact for the beta.
